Support & FAQ

How do I play?

Drag to aim (the dotted line previews bank shots) and release to shoot. Match three or more balls of the same color to pop them. Balls that are cut off from the top fall. Clear the top row, or free the trapped Lil’ Hoopers, before you run out of shots.

How do lives work?

You can hold up to 5 lives. Starting a level uses one life; you get it back if you win. A life refills every 30 minutes, even if the app is closed. If you are out of lives, you can wait, refill all lives for coins in the shop, or watch an optional video for one extra life.

I ran out of shots

You can buy +5 shots for coins (the price goes up if you buy again in the same attempt), watch one optional video per attempt, or give up and return to the map. Giving up after a level has started uses that level’s life.

How do I restore purchases?

Open the Shop or the Hoop Pop VIP screen and tap Restore Purchases. This restores the one-time Starter Pack and an active VIP subscription on this Apple ID. Consumable coins that you already spent cannot be restored. You must be signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy.

Hoop Pop VIP

VIP is an auto-renewing subscription for $2.99 per month. It lets you skip videos so rewards are instant, plus 1 hour of unlimited lives each day, a weekly booster pack, and a VIP badge. Payment is charged to your Apple Account. It renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in iOS Settings > your name > Subscriptions, or at apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.
