Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This policy describes how Hoop Pop (“the app,” “we”) handles information. Hoop Pop is a single-player game for iPhone and iPad published by Joseph Zurica.

What we collect

The developer does not collect, receive, or store your personal information on our own servers. Hoop Pop does not have accounts, and we do not ask you to create one. We do not operate a backend that receives your game progress, contacts, or payment details.

Your progress — levels, stars, coins, lives, daily rewards, settings, and similar game state — is saved locally on your device using Apple’s UserDefaults. It stays on that device until you delete the app or use Reset progress in Settings.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (coin packs, a one-time Starter Pack, and the Hoop Pop VIP monthly subscription) are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We never receive your Apple ID password or payment card details. Apple provides the app with purchase entitlements so we can unlock what you bought (for example, coins or VIP). That purchase history is used only to make those features work, not for advertising.

You can restore previous purchases with the Restore Purchases button on the Shop and VIP screens.

Advertising

Hoop Pop shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. Ads never play by themselves. You choose to watch a video, for example for an extra life or extra shots. Hoop Pop VIP subscribers do not watch ads; those rewards are granted instantly.

The first time you choose to watch a video, iOS may show Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you allow tracking, AdMob may use your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA) and related advertising data to show more relevant ads. You receive the same reward whether you allow tracking or not. You can change this later in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

When an ad is shown, Google may collect information on its own systems, such as:

  • Identifiers (device ID / advertising ID)
  • Advertising data
  • Usage data, such as product interaction with the ad
  • Diagnostics or crash data related to the ad SDK
  • Coarse location, if Google can infer it

Google may use this information for third-party advertising and advertising analytics, and for tracking if you have allowed tracking. We do not control Google’s processing. See Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses data in AdMob.

Children

Hoop Pop is a general-audience game. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will delete it to the extent we can. Parents and guardians can use Apple’s Screen Time, Ask to Buy, and Tracking settings to limit purchases and tracking.

Links and other companies

The app links to this Privacy Policy and to Apple’s standard Terms of Use, which open in Safari. Apple and Google have their own privacy policies for the services they provide (App Store payments, iCloud-style account services, and ads).

Retention and your choices

Local game data remains on your device until you delete the app or reset progress. To stop optional ads, you can decline to watch them, or subscribe to Hoop Pop VIP. To limit ad tracking, use the iOS Tracking prompt or Settings. To cancel a subscription, use your Apple Account subscription settings.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. The current version will always be posted at this URL.
