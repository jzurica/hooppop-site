Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This policy describes how Hoop Pop (“the app,” “we”) handles information. Hoop Pop is a single-player game for iPhone and iPad published by Joseph Zurica.

What we collect

The developer does not collect, receive, or store your personal information on our own servers. Hoop Pop does not have accounts, and we do not ask you to create one. We do not operate a backend that receives your game progress, contacts, or payment details.

Your progress — levels, stars, coins, lives, daily rewards, settings, and similar game state — is saved locally on your device using Apple’s UserDefaults. It stays on that device until you delete the app or use Reset progress in Settings.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (coin packs, a one-time Starter Pack, and the Hoop Pop VIP monthly subscription) are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We never receive your Apple ID password or payment card details. Apple provides the app with purchase entitlements so we can unlock what you bought (for example, coins or VIP). That purchase history is used only to make those features work, not for advertising.

You can restore previous purchases with the Restore Purchases button on the Shop and VIP screens.

Advertising

Hoop Pop shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. Ads never play by themselves. You choose to watch a video, for example for an extra life or extra shots. Hoop Pop VIP subscribers skip those videos; the same rewards are granted instantly.

Hoop Pop does not track users and does not show Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. We do not request access to the advertising identifier (IDFA) for tracking.

Every ad request is configured as child-directed and for users under the age of consent, limited to G-rated (General) ad content, and non-personalized. That means Google should not serve personalized or remarketing ads, and should not use an advertising identifier to track you across apps and websites.

When an ad is shown, Google may still collect limited information on its own systems for delivering the ad and for fraud prevention, such as:

  • Identifiers (a device identifier)
  • Advertising data
  • Diagnostics or crash data related to the ad SDK

This information is not used by Hoop Pop for tracking. We do not control Google’s processing. See Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses data in AdMob.

Children

Hoop Pop is a family-friendly game. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children, on our own servers, and the app has no accounts. Ads are always treated as child-directed and non-personalized, as described above. Parents and guardians can use Apple’s Screen Time and Ask to Buy to limit purchases. Hoop Pop is not enrolled in Apple’s Kids Category.

Links and other companies

The app links to this Privacy Policy and to Apple’s standard Terms of Use, which open in Safari. Apple and Google have their own privacy policies for the services they provide (App Store payments, iCloud-style account services, and ads).

Retention and your choices

Local game data remains on your device until you delete the app or reset progress. To skip optional ads, you can decline to watch them, or subscribe to Hoop Pop VIP (rewards are then instant, with no video). To cancel a subscription, use your Apple Account subscription settings.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. The current version will always be posted at this URL.
